    How to play

    • Click or tap a swatch to guess it - you'll get a closeness score (%).
    • Right-click (or long-press on a phone) a swatch to rule it out; it darkens with a red ✕. Tap again to un-flag. It's free.
    • Guesses are unlimited - there's no losing. Aim to solve at or under the day's par; solve it first try for a hole in one.
    • Closeness uses ΔE2000 - how different two colours look to the eye. 100% is the answer.
    • Everyone on the same date gets the same colour - a new one unlocks each day at midnight (see the timer for your reset).
    • 🎮 Play a friend to challenge someone to a live head-to-head - share a link or a 6-digit code.
    • A fresh colour unlocks each day - no skipping ahead. Name the Hue Pro (in Settings) adds a Hard Mode where you mix the colour yourself, plus an archive of past days.
